    How this library is kept

    Every entry was published in a peer-reviewed journal in 2025 or 2026, and each one was checked against the publisher's DOI record before it was listed.

    • Free to read means the journal's own version is open. Free copy means the journal version needs a subscription, but the authors posted a legal copy (arXiv, PubMed Central or a university archive), and the Read button opens that copy. An author copy can differ slightly from the final journal text.
    • Preprints that have not passed peer review are left out, along with press releases and news stories.
    • The summaries are plain-language notes written for this site. They are a guide to what the paper reports, not a replacement for reading it. Where a finding is early or disputed, a note says so.
    • Saved and read marks are stored in this browser only, so they stay private and do not follow you to another device.
